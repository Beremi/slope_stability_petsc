SuperLU_DIST provides the distributed direct solve of the coarsest PMG level
(`pmg.coarse_solver = preonly_lu` on more than one active rank); without it
multi-rank coarse LU fails at setup. Single-rank or redundant coarse LU and
GAMG coarse solves do not need it.

For a secondary build with threaded PETSc sparse kernels, enable
  --with-openmp=1
  --with-openmp-kernels=1
Reuse the first build's packages via --with-hypre-dir, --with-metis-dir,
--with-parmetis-dir and --with-superlu_dist-dir in place of the corresponding
--download flags. This retains the serial HYPRE and SuperLU_DIST builds.
Use a separate PETSC_ARCH for the threaded build. See docs/building.md.
